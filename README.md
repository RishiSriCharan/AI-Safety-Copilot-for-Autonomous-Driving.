# AI Safety Copilot for Autonomous Driving

A Computer Vision and Deep Learning-Based Predictive Driver Assistance System Using CARLA Simulation.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **AI Safety Copilot** continuously monitors driving environments within the CARLA Autonomous Driving Simulator. Moving beyond traditional reactive ADAS, this system leverages Computer Vision and Deep Learning to predict hazards, assess dynamic collision risks, and provide proactive, explainable safety recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives

* **Object & Environment Perception:** Build a real-time computer vision pipeline to detect vehicles, pedestrians, lane boundaries, traffic signs, and traffic lights.


* **Tracking & Trajectory Prediction:** Implement multi-object tracking and movement forecasting for surrounding road users.


* **Dynamic Risk Assessment:** Analyze traffic scenarios to project collision risks and trigger timely safety alerts.


* **Explainable AI (XAI):** Integrate an XAI decision module delivering transparent, human-understandable reasoning for every decision.


---

## 📊 Abstract & DatasetsThis project evaluates deep learning perception and prediction algorithms by combining high-fidelity simulated sensor outputs from the CARLA Simulator with standard real-world autonomous driving benchmark datasets.  Dataset Overview:CULane Dataset: A large-scale benchmark for urban lane detection containing complex traffic scenarios, severe illumination shifts, and diverse lane structures.BDD100K Dataset: A diverse, multi-task driving dataset featuring 100,000 video clips captured across varied weather conditions, times of day, and urban environments. Used for object detection, lane marking detection, and drivable area analysis.  CARLA Telemetry: Simulated multi-modal sensor streams (RGB cameras, Depth maps, LiDAR, IMU/GNSS).
